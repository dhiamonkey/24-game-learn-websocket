How to play

Tap a number, tap an operator, then tap a second number. The two merge into one new card. Keep going until one card is left. If it reads 24, you win the round. Tap undo to step back, or reset to start the hand again.

Keyboard: 1 to 4 picks a card, + − × ÷ (or * and /) picks an operator, Backspace undoes, Esc resets.
